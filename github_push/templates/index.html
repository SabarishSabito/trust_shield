ETHICAL FOUNDATION

An unusual signal is not a verdict.

Trust Shield helps people interpret available profile information. Automated signals can be incomplete or misleading, so assessments should inform—not replace—independent judgment.

Explain the evidence

Show the observable indicators behind each finding rather than relying on an opaque, unexplained rating.

Acknowledge uncertainty

Recognize data limitations on private accounts and treat automated assessments as forensic clues, not proof.

Avoid unsupported accusations

Focus on verifiable patterns and avoid definitive or defamatory labels without official human confirmation.
